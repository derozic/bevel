# OSINT & Open Intelligence

Ethical open-source intelligence for markets, competitors, and buyer context.

## When to use

- Competitive / market scans
- Pre-meeting account research
- Verifying public claims

## Rules

- Public / permitted sources only
- No social engineering, no credential stuffing, no illegal access
- Cite sources; timestamp captures
- Separate fact vs inference
- Privacy: minimize personal data collection

## Output contract

```markdown
## OSINT Brief — YYYY-MM-DD
**Subject:** company | market | person (public role)
**Purpose:** ...

### Key findings
1. ... (source)

### Confidence
...

### Implications for Sterling / Helm
...
```
