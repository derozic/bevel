# Culture & Norms

Operating norms that keep Entity high-trust and high-output.

## When to use

- Team health issues
- Defining working agreements
- Integrating new teams / agents

## Norm seeds

- Default to written decisions
- Critique work, not people
- Ship small; learn publicly
- No hero culture for avoidable pages
- Accessibility and security are product quality
