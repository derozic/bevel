# Knowledge Retention

Preventing bus-factor disasters: docs, runbooks, and agent memory hygiene.

## When to use

- Critical knowledge in one head
- Offboarding
- After incidents that revealed tribal knowledge

## Practices

- Runbooks next to systems
- ADRs for durable decisions
- Agent SOUL/SKILL updates after major learnings
- Recorded walkthroughs for rare procedures
- Dual coverage for break-glass paths

## Output contract

```markdown
## Knowledge Plan — YYYY-MM-DD
**Domain:** ...
**Risk (bus factor):** high | medium | low

### Critical knowledge map
...

### Capture actions
| Knowledge | Format | Owner | Due |
```
