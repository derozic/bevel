# Product Strategy

AI-first product strategy: bets, positioning, and the why behind the roadmap.

## When to use

- Defining product vision / strategy narratives
- Choosing between major product bets
- Reframing a feature factory into outcome bets

## Bet frame

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Insight | User / market truth |
| Bet | What we will build / change |
| Value | Outcome for user + business |
| Evidence | What we know vs assume |
| Risk | What could invalidate |
| Kill criteria | When we stop |
| Owner | PM / Helm |

## Rules

- Prefer outcomes over output lists
- Say no explicitly — strategy is subtraction
- AI-first: model capability is core, not garnish
