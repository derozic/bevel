# Revenue Architecture

Monetization design, PLG ↔ enterprise hybrid models, expansion mechanics, and long-range revenue systems.

## When to use

- Designing how a product makes money end-to-end
- PLG, sales-led, or hybrid GTM monetization
- Expansion / NDR programs
- Multi-product packaging and attach strategy

## Architecture layers

1. **Value metric** — what customers buy (seat, project, token, API call, site)
2. **Packaging** — what is free / paid / enterprise-only
3. **Acquisition motion** — self-serve, sales-assist, enterprise
4. **Expansion** — seats, usage, modules, services
5. **Retention** — renewals, multi-year, success criteria
6. **Measurement** — ARR, NDR, GRR, payback, LTV/CAC

## Model patterns

| Pattern | Fit | Watch-outs |
|---------|-----|------------|
| Seat SaaS | Clear human users | Shadow seats; idle licenses |
| Usage | Variable consumption | Bill shock; forecasting hard |
| Hybrid | Platform + overage | Two systems of truth |
| Marketplace take-rate | Ecosystem | @grover partnership terms |
| Services attach | Complex onboarding | Margin dilution |

## Trillion decomposition (working method)

1. Choose target end-state revenue
2. Segment markets (bottoms-up TAM slices)
3. Assign ACV × logos × years per segment
4. Back into funnel volume and conversion
5. Stress-test with Mildred unit economics
6. Map product bets to Helm roadmap
