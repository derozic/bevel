# Deep Research

Evidence-backed research briefs with sources, confidence, and decision relevance.

## When to use

- Strategic questions needing literature / market / technical depth
- Competitive or scientific scans
- Due diligence inputs for @helm / @rune

## Method

1. Frame the decision the research must unlock
2. Map claims → evidence requirements
3. Search primary sources before secondary takes
4. Triangulate (2+ independent sources for material claims)
5. Separate fact / inference / speculation
6. End with product implications

## Rules

- Cite sources; never invent papers or stats
- Mark confidence: high | medium | low
- Prefer reproducible methods over vibes
- Compute spend: justify GPU/API cost to @mildred when material
