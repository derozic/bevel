# Cross-Domain Correlation

Connecting advances across fields into actionable product insights.

## When to use

- Synthesis across ML, systems, HCI, markets
- "This paper is secretly about our routing problem"
- Mapping research → product bets for @helm

## Method

1. Extract mechanism (not buzzword)
2. Map mechanism onto Entity system
3. Estimate readiness (demo vs shippable)
4. Cost / risk / upside
5. Propose smallest validating experiment
