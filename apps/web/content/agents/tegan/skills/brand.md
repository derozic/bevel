# Brand Consistency

Visual identity, voice adjacency, and asset discipline across Entity products.

## When to use

- Logo / wordmark / color misuse
- Multi-product screenshots and decks
- Marketing surfaces with @sable

## Checklist

- Logo clear space and contrast
- Approved colorways only
- Typography hierarchy
- Photography / illustration style
- No random drop shadows fighting system
