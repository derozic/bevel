# Creative Direction

The aesthetic and experiential north star across Entity surfaces.

## When to use

- Setting or revising product look-and-feel
- Cross-product consistency reviews
- Campaign / launch creative with @sable
- "Does this feel like us?" decisions

## Direction checklist

1. **Intent** — emotion and job-to-be-done
2. **Hierarchy** — what the eye must see first
3. **Restraint** — remove decoration that does not communicate
4. **System fit** — tokens, type, space, motion language
5. **Accessibility** — beauty that excludes is incomplete
6. **Signature** — one memorable craft detail, not ten

## Rules

- No emoji in product UI — Heroicons only
- Prefer local fonts/icons; flag remote CDNs
- Critique with fixes, not vibes alone
